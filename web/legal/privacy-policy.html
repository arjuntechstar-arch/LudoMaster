1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Ludo Play handles information when you use the game, its online services, and its support channels. It applies to the Android app with package name com.ludomaster.app.mobile and its associated game API.

Offline games use local storage. Online play, accounts, friends, progression, and optional purchases use our servers or the providers described below.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: an internal account identifier, display name, optional username, selected avatar, selected country or region, account status, and registration dates. Email registration also stores your email address and a password hash; we do not store your password in plain text.
  • Installation and authentication information: a randomly generated app installation identifier, platform, app version, activity times, and login/session tokens. The installation identifier is generated by Ludo Play; it is not your IMEI, SIM number, or Android advertising ID.
  • Gameplay and progression: rooms, matchmaking, match events and results, scores, rankings, rewards, missions, achievements, tournaments, season progress, virtual currency balances, and owned or equipped items.
  • Social and safety information: friend requests, room invitations, club membership and club content, messages and emoji sent during matches, notifications, blocked players, and reports you submit. Messages are sent to match participants and may be held with match session data; do not include private or sensitive information in chat.
  • Technical information: our servers and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request times, connection information, and error or security logs to deliver and protect the service. App crash diagnostics are stored locally on your device in this release and are not automatically uploaded to an analytics or crash-reporting provider.
  • Support information: information you voluntarily provide when contacting support, such as your contact details, account username, issue description, and screenshots.
  • Optional Google connections, when configured and used: Google or Play Games account identifiers, basic profile names, and authentication tokens or authorization codes needed to verify sign-in. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail messages or Google contacts.
  • Optional Google Play purchases, when enabled: product and order identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, purchase state, delivery records, and a hashed app-account reference used to prevent purchases being credited to the wrong account. Google processes payment details; Ludo Play does not receive your full card number or banking password.

3. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate accounts, restore sessions, and save your progress.
  • Run multiplayer matches, matchmaking, invitations, clubs, rankings, and rewards.
  • Deliver and verify purchases, maintain virtual balances, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, investigate abuse, and enforce game rules.
  • Diagnose service problems, secure our systems, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. What other players can see

Your display name, chosen username and avatar, selected country or region, level, rankings, and relevant game results can be visible to other players through rooms, friends, clubs, profiles, and leaderboards. Match chat and reactions are visible to the match participants. Your email address, password hash, session tokens, and payment receipts are not displayed to other players.

Internal account identifiers are used by the service to associate gameplay and requests with the correct account. They are not intended as your public display name.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or use it for personalized advertising in this release. We share or make information available only for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Other players receive the profile, gameplay, social, and chat information needed for the features you use.
  • Hosting, database, network security, backup, and support providers process information needed to operate and protect Ludo Play. Access is limited to the service they provide.
  • Google processes information when you use Google sign-in, Play Games Services, or Google Play purchases. Our backend communicates with Google to verify your identity or purchase. Google's own privacy policy also applies to Google's services.
  • We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or when necessary to protect users, prevent fraud, or investigate abuse.

6. Permissions, local storage, and optional features

The app uses an internet connection for online features and stores settings, saved offline games, session information, selected profile details, and local crash logs on your device. The browser version uses browser storage for equivalent functions. Clearing app or browser storage removes those local copies but does not delete an online account.

This release does not request access to your contacts, SMS, camera, photo library, microphone, or precise device location. Profile avatars are supplied by the game. Selecting a country or region does not enable GPS tracking.

Google connections and real-money purchases are optional and remain unavailable until configured. This release has no active third-party advertising or voice-recording integration. We will update this policy and provide any required disclosures or permissions before introducing new collection practices.

7. Security and international processing

Production app connections use HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Passwords are hashed, sessions are authenticated, and access to server data is restricted. No system can guarantee complete security; do not share passwords, login tokens, or payment credentials in game chat or support messages.

Our infrastructure and providers may process information in countries other than where you live. We use the safeguards required by applicable law for such processing.

8. Retention and account deletion

We retain account and game information while your account is active and as needed to provide the features you use. You can request account deletion from Settings > Delete Account & Data, or use the account-deletion page linked below to contact support without reinstalling the app. We may ask for limited information to verify that the account belongs to you; never send your password.

Confirmed deletion closes your existing account and removes its email and connected-provider login credentials, installation/device records, login sessions, chosen username, inventory, missions, achievements, daily rewards, season progress, personal notifications, invitations, friends and block connections. Profile names, avatar and country are replaced with Deleted User/default values, and profile levels and statistics are reset. Existing game connections are closed. Your previous credentials and session tokens cannot reopen the deleted account.

Shared clubs and rooms pass to a remaining member where possible, or are removed when empty. Championship registrations are removed before the championship starts. Shared match and already-started championship records retain internal references and results to preserve other players' games. Your profile and attributable chat fields in stored match payloads are replaced with Deleted User/default or removed values. Deletion cannot remove messages that other players already saw, copied or captured.

A minimal closed-account record, internal player references, inaccessible wallet balance and virtual-currency ledgers, purchase and receipt records, abuse reports, and relevant security records may remain for transaction reconciliation, dispute handling, fraud prevention, safety, legal obligations or shared-game integrity. These records are not used to restore your account or personal progression. They have no automatic expiry in the current service; we assess removal requests against these purposes and applicable requirements. Contact the privacy address if you need help with retained records.

Earlier copies can remain in restricted backups until the backups expire or are replaced under the hosting provider's rotation. If an older backup is restored, account deletions must be reapplied before reopening the affected accounts. Local copies can be removed by clearing app storage; local crash logs can also be cleared in Settings > Crash Diagnostics & Logs. Using online play again may create a separate new guest account. Deleting Ludo Play does not delete your Google account or Google's purchase history.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit your display name, username, and avatar in your profile; choose whether to connect Google; use offline modes; and avoid optional purchases or social features. Settings includes Export Personal Data and Delete Account & Data.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is used, or complain to your local privacy authority. Contact us through the privacy contact below to make a request.

10. Children and families

Parents and guardians should review Ludo Play's store age rating, account features, online interaction, and purchase settings before allowing a child to use it. Please avoid sharing a child's personal details in profiles or chat. If you believe a child's information has been collected in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest update. We will provide additional notice or request consent when required by applicable law.

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion assistance, contact the Ludo Play developer through the contact link below. Identify Ludo Play and your username or account email, and describe your request. Do not include passwords, full card details, or login tokens.
